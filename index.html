The studio

Fourteen years of
watching closely

AKV Photography was founded in Bangalore in 2012 by Anil Kumar Venkatesh. The studio specialises in candid photography — the kind that does not ask a room to stop and pose — alongside traditional coverage, cinematic wedding films and pre-wedding shoots.

The team travels outstation for destination weddings, shoots the muhurtham and the mehendi and the reception with the same attention, and hands the whole story back within four weeks. Photographs, films, and albums built to be opened again in twenty years.

They believe it is the tiny details that hold the best of the memories — and they photograph those details with real precision.
