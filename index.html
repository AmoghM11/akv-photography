The studio

Fourteen years of
watching closely

AKV Photography was founded in Bangalore in 2012 by Anil Kumar Venkatesh, and specialises in candid photography — the kind that does not ask a room to stop and pose. More than six hundred weddings later, that is still the whole method.

Their knowledge of the latest trends and equipment helps them preserve your precious moments in a beautiful way. Alongside the candid work runs traditional coverage, cinematic wedding films, pre-wedding shoots and hand-sequenced albums — and the team travels outstation to cover destination weddings.

They believe it is the tiny details that hold the best of the memories — and they photograph those details with real precision.
