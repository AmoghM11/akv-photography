Stories · Pre-Wedding, Engagement & Maternity

Everything that happens
before the wedding

A pre-wedding shoot is not a rehearsal. It is the only day the two of you get the camera entirely to yourselves — no muhurtham running late, no four hundred guests, nobody waiting on a photograph. We shoot those days, the engagements that follow them, and the maternity sessions that come after.

Shot as stills and as film. Every story shoot can be delivered as candid photographs, a one-minute teaser and a longer highlight film — so the family gets something to frame and something to forward.

How a story shoot runs

We scout the location
before you ever stand in it

Anil builds a storyline for the shoot first — a place, a time of day, and a reason for the two of you to be there.

Then we scout it. Light changes an hour either side of golden hour, and a location that photographs beautifully at 5pm can be unusable at 3pm. Knowing that in advance is the difference between a shoot that flows and a shoot that spends its best light waiting.

On the day we stay out of the way. You walk, you talk, you forget about us — and that is where the frames come from. The posed portraits still get made, but they get made after the candid ones, not instead of them.

  • Location scouting — we visit or verify the spot beforehand and plan around the light.
  • Candid and traditional stills — the unposed frames first, the portraits second.
  • Teaser and highlight film — a short cut you can send out, and a longer one you will keep.
  • Colour-graded delivery — matched to the grade we will use for the wedding itself.
  • Concept shoots — themed and song-led pre-wedding films, if that is what you want.
  • Outstation travel — we go where the story is, including destination shoots.

Maternity & lifestyle

The same eye for light,
at a much quieter scale

Maternity, newborn, baby shower, naming ceremony, first birthday, portfolio and fashion — in the studio, at home, or on location.

A maternity shoot is a short, unhurried session — usually an hour or two, usually somewhere you already feel comfortable.

There is no crowd to work around and no ceremony to keep up with, which means the whole session can be built around one thing: soft, directional light and a person who is allowed to be still in it.

Then the rest of it follows. The studio shoots the baby shower, the naming ceremony and the first birthday as a matter of course, as photographs and as cinematic films — and the same colour grade and retouching standard applies as it does to a wedding.

Newborn, portfolio and fashion sessions run the same way. So do threading ceremonies and house warmings, which families have been booking the studio for since the beginning.
